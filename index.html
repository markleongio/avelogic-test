Common Questions

Government Grant FAQs

Everything you need to know about PSG and SFEC for SmartHRMS.

Is SmartHRMS officially PSG pre-approved?
Yes. SmartHRMS by Avelogic is a pre-approved solution under the IMDA SMEs Go Digital programme, listed on the Business Grants Portal at go.gov.sg/bgp.
How long does PSG approval take?
PSG applications typically take 4–8 weeks once submitted correctly. Our team helps ensure your application is complete to avoid delays.
Can I migrate from another HRMS and still apply for PSG?
Yes. You can apply when migrating to SmartHRMS, as long as the same solution category has not been previously funded for your entity.
Do I need to apply separately for SFEC?
No. SFEC eligibility is automatically assessed by Enterprise Singapore. If eligible, the S$10,000 credit appears on your Business Grants Portal account automatically.
Can I stack PSG and SFEC together?
Yes — these grants are fully stackable. PSG reduces your cost by up to 50%, and SFEC can then offset up to 90% of your remaining out-of-pocket expenses.
What is the SFEC deadline?
The current SFEC has been extended and final claims must be submitted by 30 November 2026. A redesigned SFEC is expected from 2H 2026 under the Enterprise Workforce Transformation Package (EWTP).
What documents do I need for PSG?
Typically: your UEN, CorpPass access, a vendor quotation from Avelogic, ACRA business profile, and brief description of business need. Our team provides a full checklist and assists throughout.
Is there a cap on how many PSG grants I can claim?
Only one package per solution category per deployment location is supported, with an annual grant cap of S$30,000 per entity. Multiple categories can be applied for separately.
