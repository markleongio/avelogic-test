Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Browse by topic — government grants or SmartHRMS product questions.

Is SmartHRMS officially PSG pre-approved?
Yes. SmartHRMS by Avelogic is a pre-approved solution under the IMDA SMEs Go Digital programme, listed on the Business Grants Portal at go.gov.sg/bgp.
How long does PSG approval take?
PSG applications typically take 4–8 weeks once submitted correctly. Our team helps ensure your application is complete to avoid delays.
Can I migrate from another HRMS and still apply for PSG?
Yes. You can apply when migrating to SmartHRMS, as long as the same solution category has not been previously funded for your entity.
Do I need to apply separately for SFEC?
No. SFEC eligibility is automatically assessed by Enterprise Singapore. If eligible, the S$10,000 credit appears on your Business Grants Portal account automatically.
Can I stack PSG and SFEC together?
Yes — these grants are fully stackable. PSG reduces your cost by up to 50%, and SFEC can then offset up to 90% of your remaining out-of-pocket expenses.
What is the SFEC deadline?
The current SFEC has been extended and final claims must be submitted by 30 November 2026. A redesigned SFEC is expected from 2H 2026 under the Enterprise Workforce Transformation Package (EWTP).
What documents do I need for PSG?
Typically: your UEN, CorpPass access, a vendor quotation from Avelogic, ACRA business profile, and brief description of business need. Our team provides a full checklist and assists throughout.
Is there a cap on how many PSG grants I can claim?
Only one package per solution category per deployment location is supported, with an annual grant cap of S$30,000 per entity. Multiple categories can be applied for separately.
Is SmartHRMS a web-based or installed software?
SmartHRMS is 100% web-based (SaaS). There is no software to install or servers to maintain. You access it from any browser on any device — laptop, tablet or smartphone.
Does SmartHRMS support CPF and IRAS filing?
Yes. SmartHRMS automatically calculates CPF contributions by employee age band, and supports direct IRAS AIS submission for IR8A, IR8S, Appendix 8A/8B and IR21 — all within the system.
How do I migrate from my current payroll system?
Our team provides free onsite data migration assistance. Most client data can be imported via Excel templates. We handle CPF configuration and leave policy setup as part of onboarding.
Is there a limit on the number of employees?
No hard limit. SmartHRMS scales from 5-employee startups to organisations with hundreds of staff. Pricing is per employee per month, so you only pay for what you use.
What banks does SmartHRMS support for GIRO?
SmartHRMS generates GIRO files for DBS IDEAL, UOB IBG, OCBC, HSBC MRI, Maybank RC, Citibank, SMBC, MUFG, BNP, Bank of America and more.
Can employees access their payslips and leave on mobile?
Yes. The SmartHRMS eServices app is available on iOS and Android. Employees can apply leave, submit expense claims, view payslips and download their IR8A at any time.
Is our payroll data secure?
Yes. SmartHRMS is hosted on AWS Singapore with SSL A+ encryption rating, individual MS-SQL databases per client, 2FA authentication, daily incremental and weekly full backups — fully PDPA compliant.
What support do you provide after sign-up?
Avelogic provides phone and email support Monday to Friday, 9am–6pm SGT. New clients receive onsite training from dedicated system trainers, and all regulatory updates (CPF, IRAS, MOM) are applied automatically at no extra charge.
